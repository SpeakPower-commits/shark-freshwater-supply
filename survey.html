Shark Water Survey

Know where the water is before you build.

What it is

A site visit with local leaders and an underground assessment, carried out with a registered hydrogeologist partner.

Who it is for

Districts, farms, estates, schools, health centres, churches and developers.

What you receive

A written report and map, the likely depth and yield range, and the options with rough costs.

How it runs

BriefWhat you need and where.
Site visitWith local leaders.
Field assessmentUnderground survey.
Draft reportMap and options.
ReviewA meeting with you.
Final reportReady for design.

Borehole drilling needs a registered drilling contractor and a drilling permit backed by a hydrogeological survey report. We manage that process for you. A survey shows where water is likely to be found. It does not guarantee yield.
