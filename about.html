About Shark

A Ugandan freshwater supply and construction company, founded by Hassan Maweje.

Our story

Walked a thousand villages. Decided to do it differently.

Hassan Maweje has walked through more than a thousand villages in Nakasongola, Kayunga, Nakaseke, Buikwe and beyond.† In many of them he saw the same thing: families and cattle waiting for water, farms that could not be watered, and help that never quite arrived. [one specific village and moment, in Hassan’s own words]

He came to a simple conclusion. The problem is often not willingness but method. Too many water systems are built without first understanding what lies underground, and left without anyone trained to keep them running.

Survey first. Build to standard. Train local people to keep the water flowing.

† Founder-reported. Supporting field records are being compiled.

Our promise and values

Survey-led

We find the water before we build.

Standards-driven

We build to standard with permitted specialists and keep honest records.

Community-first

We employ and train local people and respect the communities we serve.

Our core values are integrity, credibility and professionalism, supported by impact and empowerment. We do not pay or accept bribes, and every payment has a receipt.
